How new lore fits into the Archives

These seven Archives preserve the foundational history of the setting. New locations, people, organizations, cultures, objects, and other lore do not need to become new numbered Archives. Instead, they belong in the Lore Index below.

Lore Index

The growing catalog of individual lore entries that make up the living world of the Verdigris Isles. This is where new additions belong.

Explore the World

Unlike the numbered Archives, the Lore Index is designed to grow. New entries can be added here without changing the structure of the main archive.
