Verde Haven

The capital city built among the remnants of the fallen Citadel, where reclaimed ruins, old gardens, and new civilization have grown together.

Related Lore

Overview

Verde Haven is the modern capital of the Verdigris Isles, built among the surviving remnants of the civilization that existed before the Fall of the Citadel.

Unlike a city founded from untouched ground, Verde Haven grew around ruins, foundations, gardens, and fragments of the old world. Old Citadel architecture exists alongside generations of new construction.

The result is a city that feels neither entirely ancient nor entirely modern. Verde Haven is a living record of the world that was lost and the civilization that followed it.

A City Built From What Survived Verde Haven's identity comes from the layers of civilization built upon one another after the Fall.

Before the Fall

Before Verde Haven existed, the land was part of the Citadel's elite residential and garden districts.

The area contained carefully maintained gardens, residences, walkways, service structures, and infrastructure designed for those who lived close to the highest levels of Citadel society.

Much of this district would eventually become buried beneath the ruins of the Citadel itself.

The Final Days

The years immediately preceding 0 FC were marked by a gradual collapse of Citadel infrastructure.

Aetherite reserves became critically depleted. Power systems became unreliable, infrastructure began failing, and resources were increasingly rationed.

The Citadel had once seemed permanent. It was not.

0 FC — The Fall

0 FC marks the physical Fall of the Citadel itself.

The events immediately preceding the catastrophe belong to the Final Days. The Fall itself is the moment when the Citadel collapsed, destroying enormous portions of the civilization that had depended upon it.

The Settlement Era

In the generations following the Fall, survivors began occupying the ruins left behind.

The fallen residential and garden districts proved especially valuable. Existing foundations offered shelter, while surviving walls and structures could be repaired rather than rebuilt from nothing.

Over generations, the settlement expanded outward from these ruins.

The Gardens

Among the most recognizable remnants of the old Citadel are the gardens that once belonged to its elite residential districts.

Some survived remarkably intact. Others were buried, overgrown, or transformed by generations of settlement.

Modern Verde Haven grew around these surviving spaces rather than completely replacing them.

Architecture

Verde Haven's architecture reflects centuries of rebuilding.

Reclaimed Citadel stonework can be found beside nineteenth- century-inspired industrial buildings, wooden residences, metal workshops, and structures incorporating salvaged magitech components.

Districts

The city is divided into districts that reflect both its history and its modern function.

The oldest portions of the city remain closely tied to the ruins of the Citadel, while newer districts expanded outward into areas that were once peripheral settlements.

Industry

Verde Haven is one of the major industrial centers of the Verdigris Isles.

Workshops, factories, foundries, printing houses, and magitech repair facilities make use of both conventional nineteenth-century technology and carefully reclaimed technology from the old world.

Transportation

Transportation throughout Verde Haven reflects the city's position as both a major trade center and a settlement shaped by its waterways.

Ships, carts, rail systems, and Drift-powered airboats connect the capital to surrounding settlements and trade routes.

Culture

Verde Haven's culture is shaped by the coexistence of old-world relics and new-world traditions.

The people of the capital live surrounded by evidence of the civilization that came before them while simultaneously building something distinctly their own.

Religion

Religious traditions in Verde Haven vary considerably between communities.

Some traditions developed after the Fall, while others preserve fragments of older beliefs.

Food & Trade

As the capital, Verde Haven serves as a major center for trade.

Produce from surrounding regions, seafood from the coast, manufactured goods, reclaimed materials, and specialized magitech components all pass through the city.

Daily Life

Daily life in Verde Haven combines familiar nineteenth-century industrial living with the strange conveniences of reclaimed magitech.

Markets, workshops, public gardens, waterways, factories, and residential neighborhoods all exist within relatively close proximity.

Population

Verde Haven is one of the most culturally diverse settlements in the Verdigris Isles.

People from across the archipelago pass through the capital for trade, work, education, politics, and exploration.

Factions

The capital contains representatives of numerous political, commercial, cultural, and adventuring organizations.

Some operate openly within the city while others maintain smaller networks hidden among its districts.
