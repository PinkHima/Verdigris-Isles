Overview

Verde Haven is the modern capital of the Verdigris Isles, built among the surviving remnants of the world that existed before the Fall of the Citadel.

Unlike cities founded from untouched ground, Verde Haven grew around ruins, gardens, foundations, and fragments of the old world. The result is a city where reclaimed architecture and new construction exist side by side.

History

The history of Verde Haven begins before the city itself existed. The area was once part of the Citadel's elite residential and garden districts.

When the Citadel fell, portions of these districts were carried down with it. The ruins became the foundation upon which later generations built their homes.

The Fall

0 FC marks the physical Fall of the Citadel itself. The years leading up to this event belong to the Final Days, when the Citadel's infrastructure was already beginning to fail.

Power became unreliable. Resources were rationed. Systems that had once been considered permanent began to fail one after another.

Then the Citadel came down.

The First Settlement

In the generations following the Fall, survivors discovered that the ruins could provide something the open wilderness could not: shelter.

The fallen residential districts became particularly valuable. Walls, foundations, gardens, and surviving structures were adapted rather than destroyed.

The Gardens

Among the most remarkable remnants of the old Citadel are the elite gardens that once surrounded its residential districts.

Some sections survived the Fall remarkably well. Others were buried beneath debris and slowly reclaimed by vegetation.

Modern Verde Haven was built around these surviving spaces, making the gardens an unusual bridge between the old world and the new.

Modern Verde Haven

Today, Verde Haven is a thriving capital whose architecture reflects centuries of rebuilding.

Old Citadel structures stand beside nineteenth-century-inspired industrial construction, reclaimed magitech machinery, markets, workshops, gardens, and residential districts.

The city is neither entirely old nor entirely new. Its identity comes from the layers between the two.
