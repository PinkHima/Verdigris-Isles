About the setting

A World of Sun, Sea & Sorcery

Verdigris Isles is a post-Ascension high-fantasy world where civilization is rebuilding among the remains of an extraordinary fallen age.

The modern Isles

Beauty above, mysteries below.

The Isles are a scattered collection of nations, cities, wildernesses, and forgotten ruins. The old world left behind impossible machines, magical relics, and secrets that modern people are only beginning to understand.

Life is shaped by the sea. Ships connect distant communities, coastal markets bustle with travelers, and remote wetlands and islands hide discoveries waiting to be made.

Beneath the relaxed beauty of island life lies a world filled with adventure, political intrigue, ancient mysteries, and the dangers of magic.

The closer you get to the old ruins, the less certain the map becomes.
Core themes

What defines Verdigris?

⚓

Maritime life

Ports, ferries, fishing villages, merchant fleets, and island-to-island journeys make the sea the setting's connective tissue.

🏛️

Ruins of progress

Ancient infrastructure and magitech survive in fragments, offering both opportunity and danger to the people who find them.

🌿

Wild frontiers

Forests, wetlands, mountains, and distant shores remain places where maps give way to discovery.
