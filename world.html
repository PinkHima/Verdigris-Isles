🌎 Regions of the Known World

Seven Lands.
Many Cultures.

Beyond the Verdigris Isles lie kingdoms, confederacies, city-states, and maritime powers shaped by radically different landscapes, histories, traditions, and ways of life.

✦ The Known World

A World of Different Ways of Life

The Verdigris Isles sit within a wider world of competing kingdoms, maritime powers, frontier nations, and ancient civilizations.

Each region has developed its own relationship with magic, technology, trade, nature, warfare, and the legacy of the Age of Ascension.

These differences influence everything from architecture and clothing to food, occupations, social customs, religion, education, and the way people understand their place in the world.

The people who arrive in Verde Haven therefore bring more than goods and currency. They bring languages, traditions, beliefs, rivalries, stories, and customs from across the known world.

✦ The Seven Regions

Where the World Meets the Isles

The nations surrounding the Verdigris Isles each possess distinct identities shaped by their geography and history.

🌊
Southwest

The Thalassan League

Government: Maritime League of Free Cities

The Thalassan League is a coalition of prosperous port cities united through commerce, naval defense, and exploration.

Its influence stretches across the seas through merchant fleets, trade agreements, and financial power rather than territorial conquest. Every major shipping route eventually passes through a Thalassan harbor.

⛰️
Northwest

The Kharad Highlands

Government: High Kingdom

The Kharad Highlands are a rugged realm of mountain fortresses, deep valleys, and ancient mines.

Rather than pursuing the wonders of Skyglass during the Age of Ascension, the Kharadi perfected metallurgy, engineering, and mechanical craftsmanship.

🌿
Northeast

Ardynwood

Government: Constitutional Monarchy

Ardynwood is an ancient woodland kingdom where civilization has grown alongside towering forests instead of replacing them.

Its cities are woven into colossal trees and connected by elevated walkways, rope bridges, and living architecture cultivated over generations.

☀️
North

The Heliora Compact

Government: Confederation of City-States

The Heliora Compact is a confederation of prosperous desert city-states devoted to scholarship, astronomy, mathematics, and magical research.

Home to renowned universities, observatories, and libraries, Heliora has preserved much of the world's surviving knowledge.

🌺
East

The Azure Confederacy

Government: Island Confederation

The Azure Confederacy is a federation of tropical island nations united through trade, culture, and mutual defense.

Its people are renowned for generations of maritime tradition, pearl diving, coral architecture, and sustainable ocean living.

🐾
Southeast

The Talanor Dominion

Government: Constitutional Monarchy

Beyond the settled trade routes lies the Talanor Dominion, a rugged frontier nation of towering cliffs, windswept highlands, dense forests, and untamed wilderness.

Rather than seeking wealth through commerce, Talanor has built its reputation on resilience, exploration, and survival.

🏛️
West

The Caelmont Court

Government: Noble Court

The Caelmont Court is one of the oldest and most influential realms in the known world, governed by an intricate network of noble houses.

Court life is an art unto itself, with families competing for influence through lavish galas, patronage, arranged marriages, carefully cultivated reputations, and political maneuvering.

✦ Southwest · Maritime Culture

The Thalassan League

A culture built around the sea, commerce, and the belief that the world's waters connect rather than divide its peoples.

🌊 A Life on the Water

Thalassan culture revolves around maritime life. Ports serve as centers of commerce, politics, social life, and cultural exchange.

Families often have long histories connected to particular ships, harbors, merchant houses, or naval traditions.

⚓ Merchant Traditions

Commerce is viewed as both an occupation and an art. Successful merchants are expected to understand negotiation, navigation, diplomacy, finance, and foreign customs.

Merchant guilds hold considerable cultural influence throughout League society.

🧭 Exploration

Exploration is highly respected. Mapping an unfamiliar coastline, discovering a new route, or returning with knowledge from distant waters can bring enormous prestige.

🗺️ Navigation as Heritage

Navigation knowledge is frequently passed between generations. Experienced sailors teach younger family members to read currents, stars, winds, reefs, and coastlines.

Social Ideal The Thalassan ideal is the capable traveler: someone who can enter an unfamiliar port, understand its people, negotiate fairly, and find their way home.

✦ Northwest · Mountain Culture

The Kharad Highlands

A culture shaped by mountains, craftsmanship, engineering, and the pride of building things meant to endure.

⛰️ Mountain Communities

Kharadi settlements are built around valleys, mines, fortresses, and mountain passes. Communities tend to be tightly connected because surviving the Highlands has historically required cooperation.

🔨 Craftsmanship

Craftsmanship carries enormous cultural prestige. A person's work is often considered a reflection of their character, patience, and dedication.

Master smiths, engineers, builders, and mechanics are respected members of society.

⚙️ Practical Innovation

Kharadi culture favors useful innovation over spectacle. Machinery is valued when it solves a problem, improves safety, or allows people to accomplish something previously impossible.

🛡️ The Fortress Ideal

Fortification is more than military necessity. Strong walls, reliable bridges, secure roads, and enduring buildings represent stability and responsibility.

Social Ideal The Kharadi ideal is the builder: someone whose skill leaves the world stronger than they found it.

✦ Northeast · Woodland Culture

Ardynwood

An ancient woodland civilization built around coexistence with the natural world rather than its replacement.

🌿 Living Architecture

Ardynwood settlements are cultivated rather than simply constructed. Homes, walkways, halls, and gathering places may be grown around living trees over generations.

🦌 Wardens

Respected wardens protect forests, settlements, and wildlife while helping communities maintain a balance with the natural environment.

🌙 Spirit Traditions

Spiritual traditions often emphasize the relationships between people, animals, forests, rivers, and the unseen forces believed to inhabit the natural world.

🌱 Local Governance

Though Ardynwood has a monarch, villages and local communities retain significant authority through councils and respected community leaders.

Social Ideal The Ardynwood ideal is the caretaker: someone who protects what they inherit so that those who come after them may inherit it as well.

✦ North · Desert Culture

The Heliora Compact

A civilization where scholarship, astronomy, mathematics, and magical research form the foundation of society.

☀️ Desert City-States

Helioran cities rise from the desert as independent centers of learning, trade, and political influence. Their cooperation allows the Compact to function without requiring a single centralized capital.

📚 Knowledge as Legacy

Books, records, mathematical discoveries, magical research, and historical documents are treated as cultural treasures.

Libraries are often among the most important institutions in a Helioran city.

🔭 The Night Sky

Astronomy is deeply embedded within Helioran life. Scholars study celestial movements for scientific, navigational, agricultural, and magical purposes.

✨ Magical Scholarship

Magic is approached as something worthy of careful study. Helioran researchers document magical phenomena and seek to understand the principles behind them.

Social Ideal The Helioran ideal is the seeker of knowledge: someone who leaves behind ignorance and adds something meaningful to the world's understanding.

✦ East · Island Culture

The Azure Confederacy

A tropical maritime civilization built around cooperation, ocean traditions, and sustainable relationships with the sea.

🌺 Island Communities

Azure society is spread across numerous island nations, each with its own customs and traditions while participating in the wider Confederacy.

🤝 Cooperation

The Confederacy's strength comes from mutual defense and shared interests rather than conquest. Cooperation between islands is treated as essential to survival.

🐚 Life Beneath the Waves

Pearl diving, fishing, shell gathering, and coral craftsmanship are deeply connected to everyday life.

🌊 Sustainable Living

Azure traditions emphasize taking from the ocean without destroying the environments that sustain island communities.

Social Ideal The Azure ideal is the community member: someone who understands that an island survives through the strength and cooperation of everyone upon it.

✦ Southeast · Frontier Culture

The Talanor Dominion

A rugged frontier nation where survival, exploration, and knowledge of the wilderness are valued above wealth and comfort.

🐾 Frontier Life

Talanorian settlements exist alongside vast stretches of wilderness. People are accustomed to traveling long distances and dealing with environments where roads and established settlements may suddenly disappear.

🏹 Rangers & Hunters

Rangers, scouts, hunters, and monster hunters occupy an important place in society. Their knowledge can mean the difference between life and death for isolated communities.

🦅 Beast Handling

Falconry and the training of useful or dangerous beasts are respected traditions. Animals are valued as companions, working partners, and sources of knowledge about the wilderness.

🗺️ Uncharted Lands

Mapping unexplored territory is considered a worthwhile pursuit. Talanorian cartographers frequently record landmarks, monster territories, resources, and safe routes.

Social Ideal The Talanorian ideal is the survivor: someone capable of facing the unknown, adapting to hardship, and bringing others safely home.

✦ West · Court Culture

The Caelmont Court

A realm where prestige, lineage, diplomacy, artistry, and political influence shape nearly every aspect of high society.

🏛️ The Great Houses

Caelmont society revolves around its noble houses. Family lineage, reputation, alliances, and accumulated influence determine a person's position within courtly society.

🎭 Courtly Performance

Social interaction is carefully cultivated. Clothing, speech, manners, invitations, gifts, and appearances may all carry political meaning.

💍 Alliances & Marriage

Marriage can be a powerful political instrument. Arranged marriages, strategic courtships, and family alliances have historically shaped relationships between noble houses.

🎨 Patronage

Wealthy families compete to sponsor artists, scholars, architects, performers, and craftspeople. Exceptional work can elevate both the creator and the house that supports them.

Social Ideal The Caelmont ideal is the cultivated individual: someone who understands etiquette, reputation, influence, and the importance of leaving an unforgettable impression.

✦ The Verdigris Connection

Why They Come to the Isles

The Verdigris Isles occupy a unique position in the modern world. Every surrounding power has its own reasons for watching, trading with, studying, or exploring the islands.

🌊 Thalassan League Sponsors expeditions into the Crownscar in search of lost Ascension navigation technology while maintaining lucrative trade routes with Verde Haven.
⛰️ Kharad Highlands Kharadi engineers travel to the Isles to study ancient Ascension technology and adapt forgotten designs using modern materials and craftsmanship.
🌿 Ardynwood Herbalists, druids, and naturalists accompany Crownscar expeditions to study how Skyglass and ancient relics continue to affect surrounding ecosystems.
☀️ Heliora Compact Helioran scholars journey to the Isles seeking forgotten knowledge hidden beneath the Citadel's ruins, believing the Crownscar may hold answers about the Age of Ascension.
🌺 Azure Confederacy Azure traders and travelers are among the most frequent visitors to Verde Haven, carrying goods, cultures, and stories from across the known world.
🐾 Talanor Dominion Talanorian rangers and explorers are frequently recruited by the Adventurer's Guild to guide expeditions through the Crownscar and the wilderness of the Verdigris Isles.
🏛️ Caelmont Court Great houses sponsor Crownscar expeditions to acquire relics, lost histories, and priceless artifacts that can elevate their prestige within the Grand Court.
✦ Across the World

Different Places, Different Values

No single culture represents the known world. Each region has developed its own answer to what makes a person valuable.

🌊 Thalassan

Adaptability, commerce, navigation, and the ability to connect different peoples.

⛰️ Kharadi

Skill, craftsmanship, durability, engineering, and building things that endure.

🌿 Ardynwood

Stewardship, balance, community responsibility, and protection of the natural world.

☀️ Helioran

Knowledge, scholarship, discovery, mathematics, and the pursuit of understanding.

🌺 Azure

Cooperation, community, maritime tradition, and sustainable relationships with the ocean.

🐾 Talanorian

Resilience, survival, exploration, independence, and knowledge of dangerous places.

🏛️ Caelmont

Prestige, lineage, diplomacy, reputation, artistry, and political influence.

🌎 Verdigris

Adaptation, discovery, reconstruction, and the opportunity to build something new from the remnants of the past.

✦ Character Backgrounds

Where Were You Raised?

A character's homeland can influence their upbringing, traditions, worldview, education, and relationship with the wider world.

🌊 Thalassan

Consider a childhood surrounded by ships, ports, merchants, sailors, and constant travelers.

⛰️ Kharadi

Consider growing up around mountains, workshops, mines, fortresses, engineers, and master craftsmen.

🌿 Ardynwood

Consider a childhood within a forest community where nature, spirits, local councils, and wardens shape everyday life.

☀️ Helioran

Consider being raised around scholars, libraries, observatories, universities, mathematics, and magical research.

🌺 Azure

Consider an island upbringing shaped by sailing, fishing, pearl diving, coral architecture, and strong community ties.

🐾 Talanorian

Consider learning survival skills from an early age and growing up around rangers, hunters, explorers, and dangerous wilderness.

🏛️ Caelmont

Consider being raised within noble society, where etiquette, reputation, family alliances, education, and political influence shape daily life.

🌿 Verdigris Isles

Consider growing up in Verde Haven or another island settlement where cultures from across the world meet amid reconstruction and relic exploration.
