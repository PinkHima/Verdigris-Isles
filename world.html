The Setting

Built Around the Shore

Life here follows the coastline. Fishing villages, beach settlements, busy ports, jungle paths, and secluded coves connect the people of the archipelago.

But the beauty hides an older world. Beneath the palms and turquoise water are ruins, relics, strange machines, and traces of the Aether that powered civilization before the fall.

A quiet afternoon on a beach can become the beginning of an expedition into something much older.

Beyond the palms...

Follow the rocks. Cross the shallows. Find the old path through the greenery. Every island has somewhere the map forgot.
