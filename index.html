The Setting

Built Around the Shore

Verdigris Isles should feel warm, lush, colorful, and explorable—like an illustrated island adventure book with fantasy hidden beneath the surface.

Life here follows the coastline. Fishing villages, beach settlements, busy ports, jungle paths, and secluded coves connect the people of the archipelago.

But the beauty hides an older world. Beneath the palms and turquoise water are ruins, relics, strange machines, and traces of the Aether that powered civilization before the fall.

A quiet afternoon on a beach can become the beginning of an expedition into something much older.

Beyond the palms...

Follow the rocks. Cross the shallows. Find the old path through the greenery. Every island has somewhere the map forgot.

The Archipelago

Islands, Shores & Wild Places

From colorful coastal cities to untouched wetlands and dangerous ruins, every region has its own character.

Verde Haven

A lively coastal capital filled with markets, cafés, workshops, guilds, ships, and travelers.

🏛️

Crownscar

The dangerous ruins created by the fall of the Citadel, where relics and magical anomalies remain.

🌿

Elara Wilds

Lush forests, wetlands, rivers, and wild coastlines watched over by the Elara Wardens.

⛰️

Kharad Highlands

Rugged mountain country known for metallurgy, engineering, mining, and skilled craftspeople.

⚓

Azure Coast

A maritime region of ports, beaches, fishing communities, trade routes, and the Azure Confederacy.

🌅

Talanor Expanse

A broad frontier where settlements and exploration continue toward lands still only partly known.
