🌿 Peoples of the Verdigris Isles

Beastials

Animal-born peoples descended from countless ancestral lineages, Beastials are among the most widespread peoples of the Verdigris Isles. Their forms reflect the creatures from which their ancestors descended, creating an extraordinary diversity of appearances and adaptations.

✦ Identity

Many Beasts. One People.

While all Beastials share humanoid intelligence and physiology, each lineage carries the distinct features of the animals from which their ancestors descended.

Some Beastials possess distinctly animalistic bodies, while others retain an overwhelmingly humanoid appearance with only a few defining traits. Fur, feathers, scales, tails, ears, horns, claws, wings, and other features can all be part of a Beastial's natural appearance.

Beastials are divided into two primary races: Ferans and Kemori.

✦ The Two Races

Two Expressions of Ancestral Blood

Feran and Kemori describe broad physical expressions of Beastial ancestry rather than limiting the enormous variety of lineages found throughout the Isles.

🐺

Feran

Animalistic

Ferans possess a more animalistic appearance, often retaining digitigrade legs, muzzles, tails, extensive fur or scales, and other pronounced animal features.

They retain more of their ancestral bloodline, accounting for their more pronounced physical characteristics.

Some Ferans may also display unique Aether manifestations associated with their lineage.

🦅

Kemori

Humanoid

Kemori retain a predominantly humanoid form while displaying the defining traits of their lineage through ears, tails, wings, horns, plumage, scales, or other characteristics.

Their animal heritage may be immediately recognizable or expressed through subtler physical traits.

✦ Lineages

Countless Creatures. Countless Forms.

Beastial ancestry can come from an enormous range of animal life found throughout the Verdigris Isles.

🐺

Canines

Wolves, foxes, dogs, coyotes, and other canine lineages may display heightened senses, ears, tails, fur, claws, or other canine traits.

🐆

Great Cats

Felines may possess expressive ears, tails, patterned coats, retractable claws, keen eyesight, and other predatory adaptations.

🦌

Cervines

Deer and related lineages are often associated with antlers, sensitive hearing, keen senses, and strong relationships with forests and wild places.

🐍

Reptiles

Reptilian Beastials may display scales, tails, claws, slit pupils, horns, fins, or other traits associated with their ancestry.

🐸

Amphibians

Amphibian lineages may possess webbing, specialized skin, unusual coloration, powerful jumping ability, or other adaptations.

🦋

Insects

Insect lineages may display chitin, antennae, compound-like eyes, wings, additional limbs, or other distinctive traits.

🐟

Marine Lineages

Aquatic Beastial lineages may possess fins, scales, gills, aquatic coloration, webbing, or other traits suited to life in or around water.

🪶

Harpies

Feathered Kemori whose most recognizable feature is a pair of hip-mounted wings. Harpies are among the most distinctive Beastial lineages found throughout the Isles.

✦ Overview & Origins

Ancestors Written in Fur and Feather

The true origins of the Beastials remain uncertain.

Ancient myths claim they were shaped by the world's primordial spirits, each lineage blessed with the traits of a different creature.

Scholars instead believe Beastials descended from ancient peoples whose forms gradually adapted to the environments they inhabited over countless generations.

Whatever their beginning, Beastials have existed since before recorded history and can be found in nearly every nation of the Verdigris Isles.

✦ Physical Characteristics

No Single Beastial Appearance

Every Beastial lineage is unique, though all possess a fundamentally humanoid body plan.

🐕

Canine Traits

Ears, tails, muzzles, claws, fur, and heightened senses are common among canine lineages.

🐈

Feline Traits

Feline Beastials may possess distinctive eyes, tails, ears, fur patterns, and retractable claws.

🦌

Antlers & Horns

Antlers and horns are common among cervine, bovine, caprine, and numerous other Beastial lineages.

🐊

Scales & Fins

Reptilian and aquatic lineages may display scales, fins, tails, claws, or other specialized anatomy.

🦋

Chitin & Antennae

Insect lineages may develop chitinous plating, antennae, wings, unusual eyes, and other arthropod features.

🪶

Wings & Plumage

Feathered lineages such as Harpies may possess wings, plumage, talons, beaks, and other avian characteristics.

✦ Culture & Society

More Than the Beast Within

Beastials are deeply diverse and rarely organize themselves by race alone. Most identify more strongly with their homeland, family, or lineage than with simply being Beastial.

Lineage Family ancestry and inherited traditions may influence culture without defining an individual's identity.
Homeland Beastial cultures are often shaped more strongly by the region and communities where they were raised.
Tradition Festivals, rites of passage, and family customs may reflect ancestral instincts or natural behaviors.
Individuality Ancestry influences appearance and culture, but never determines personality, beliefs, or morality.
✦ Common Occupations

Every Calling Is Open

Beastials thrive in virtually every profession throughout the Verdigris Isles. Certain lineages may develop cultural associations with particular fields, but these are tendencies rather than limitations.

Wardens Guards Couriers Scouts Navigators Aerial Hunters Foresters Herbalists Explorers Scholars Relic Hunters Traders Craftspeople Artists Adventurers

Harpies, for example, often become couriers, scouts, navigators, or aerial hunters. Canines frequently serve as Wardens or guards, cervines as foresters and herbalists, while corvid Harpies are renowned explorers, scholars, and relic hunters.

These are cultural tendencies rather than limitations. Any Beastial may pursue any calling.

✦ Lifespan & Biology

Born of Many Ancestries

Most Beastials mature at roughly the same rate as humans and typically live between 110 and 130 years, though some lineages may naturally live slightly longer or shorter depending on their ancestry.

Despite their physical diversity, all Beastials are warm-blooded humanoids capable of living comfortably alongside the other peoples of the Verdigris Isles.
