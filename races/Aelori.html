🌊 Peoples of the Verdigris Isles

Aelori

Sea-born peoples descended from countless aquatic and semi-aquatic ancestral lineages, the Aelori are among the most widespread peoples throughout the waters and waterways of the Verdigris Isles.

✦ Identity

Children of the Water

While all Aelori share humanoid intelligence and a common aquatic heritage, their bodies and adaptations vary greatly depending on their ancestral lineage and environment.

An Aelori may possess fins, scales, gills, tails, shells, webbed limbs, bioluminescent markings, specialized senses, amphibious skin, or other adaptations shaped by the waters in which their ancestors lived.

No single Aelori appearance is considered typical. Two members of the same branch may look radically different while still sharing the same broader cultural and ancestral identity.

✦ The Three Branches

Three Waters. Countless Lineages.

Aelori are divided into three primary branches based largely on the environments their ancestral lineages adapted to.

🌊

Oceanic

Open Water

Oceanic Aelori are adapted to the open ocean, deep waters, reefs, and marine environments.

They often possess pronounced aquatic traits such as fins, tails, gills, scales, bioluminescence, specialized senses, or other adaptations suited to life beneath the waves.

Common Lineages:
Sharks · Octopi · Squid · Eels · Rays · Jellyfish · Cetaceans · Crustaceans · Other Marine Creatures

🏝️

Tidal

Coast & Shore

Tidal Aelori are adapted to coastlines, shallows, reefs, islands, and the boundary between land and sea.

They tend to retain more humanoid forms than many Oceanic lineages, though aquatic traits remain common.

Their communities frequently maintain strong relationships with coastal settlements and surface-dwelling peoples.

🐊

Miren

Freshwater

Miren Aelori are adapted to freshwater environments, rivers, marshes, swamps, wetlands, and floodplains.

Their lineages often possess traits associated with creatures that thrive where land and water overlap.

Common Lineages:
Crocodilians · Turtles · Frogs · Salamanders · Axolotls · Newts · Other Freshwater Creatures

✦ Recognized Variants

Distinct Traditions Within the Tides

Certain Aelori lineages have developed recognizable traits and cultural traditions that cross individual family lines.

🐟

Merrow

Fishlike Aelori with humanoid upper bodies and aquatic tails. Merrow are among the most recognizable Aelori to surface-dwellers.

🎶

Siren

Aelori known for powerful voices and vocal abilities. Depending on the individual, Sirens may possess enchanting voices, vocal magic, hypnotic abilities, exceptional hearing, or remarkable musical talent.

🦭

Rhaeren

Aelori capable of assuming an animal form through a bonded animal hide. The hide is an important part of Rhaeren identity and is considered an extension of the individual rather than ordinary clothing.

Common Hides:
Seal · Sea Lion · Otter · Walrus · Other Aquatic Animals

✦ Overview & Origins

Born of Ancient Waters

The origins of the Aelori are as uncertain as the depths of the seas they inhabit.

Ancient coastal traditions claim the first Aelori emerged from the primordial waters, shaped by the sea itself and given forms suited to its countless environments.

Scholars offer less poetic explanations, suggesting that the Aelori descended from ancient aquatic peoples whose bodies gradually adapted to different waters and environments over countless generations.

Today, Aelori communities can be found throughout the seas surrounding the Verdigris Isles, along its coasts, within its rivers, and deep within its marshlands.

✦ Physical Characteristics

No Two Waters Shape the Same Body

Every Aelori lineage is unique, though all possess a fundamentally humanoid body plan.

🦈

Marine Anatomy

Sharklike teeth, fins, tails, specialized senses, scales, and other adaptations are common among Oceanic lineages.

🐙

Cephalopod Traits

Tentacles, chromatophores, unusual skin textures, and other cephalopod characteristics may appear among certain lineages.

✨

Bioluminescence

Some Aelori possess naturally glowing markings, organs, or patterns adapted to life in darker waters.

🦀

Armor & Shells

Shells, claws, armored plates, and other protective anatomy appear among numerous aquatic lineages.

🐸

Amphibious Traits

Webbed hands and feet, amphibious skin, regenerative traits, and freshwater adaptations are particularly common among Miren.

🫧

Aquatic Respiration

Depending on lineage, Aelori may possess gills or other adaptations allowing them to breathe underwater indefinitely.

✦ Culture & Society

Water Is Home. Water Is History.

Aelori culture is shaped first and foremost by water. Where an Aelori lives, how they travel, and which waters they consider their own often carries greater significance than their specific lineage.

Oceanic Communities Often mobile, following currents, seasonal migrations, marine life, and shifting resources.
Tidal Communities Often maintain permanent relationships with coastal settlements as traders, navigators, guides, and intermediaries.
Miren Communities Frequently develop around rivers, marshes, and wetlands, with settlements following waterways rather than roads.
Navigation & Memory Experienced navigators may memorize entire underwater routes using currents, rocks, ruins, reefs, and wildlife.
✦ Customs & Traditions

The Things the Water Remembers

💧

Water Names

Some Aelori receive a personal name during childhood and later earn a second name associated with a significant body of water, journey, or accomplishment.

🧭

Currentkeeping

Experienced navigators preserve knowledge of dangerous waters and hidden routes, often teaching them orally rather than recording them on maps.

🌊

First Dive

Many communities mark adulthood with a ceremonial first journey into deeper or more dangerous waters.

🤝

Watersharing

Offering another Aelori access to one's waters is traditionally considered an important gesture of trust and hospitality.

🌙

Tide Gatherings

Significant tides bring communities together for trade, celebrations, courtship, storytelling, and the exchange of news.

✨

Driftmarks

Some Aelori leave subtle physical or magical markers along underwater routes to communicate warnings, directions, or territorial boundaries.

✦ Common Occupations

Those Who Know the Water

Aelori often excel in professions that take advantage of environments inaccessible to other peoples.

Navigators Siren Guides Divers Salvagers Pearl Collectors Shell Collectors Fisherfolk River Pilots Coastal Traders Shipwrights Relic Hunters Couriers Cartographers Tidewardens Smugglers Explorers
✦ Lifespan & Biology

Adapted to Land and Sea

Most Aelori typically live between 140 and 210 years, though individual lineages may naturally live somewhat longer or shorter depending on their ancestry.

Their biology varies considerably between branches. Some Aelori are capable of breathing underwater indefinitely, while others can comfortably transition between aquatic and terrestrial environments.

Certain lineages are almost entirely aquatic, while others can spend most of their lives on land.

Despite these differences, all Aelori possess humanoid-level intelligence and are capable of interacting with the wider societies of the Verdigris Isles.
