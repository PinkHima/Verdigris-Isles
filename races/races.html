People of the Isles

Many Shores. Many Lineages.

The peoples of Verdigris have adapted to mountains, wetlands, forests, beaches, and bustling island cities.

👤

Humans

Adaptable and widespread, humans can be found throughout nearly every settlement and region.

🐾

Beastials

Animal-born peoples descended from countless lineages, including Ferans and Kemori.

🧜‍♀️

Aelori

Aquatic peoples of the water with Oceanic, Tidal, and Miren variants.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Duvara

A shapeshifting people with Wild forms ranging from traditional animal forms to mythical and veiled variants.

🦋

Elyri

The Elements Given Form. The Elyri embody the fundamental elements and are divided into four branches: Ignari, Aquari, Terrani, and Tempesti.

✨

Other Lineages

Unique races and cultures can be developed around the environments and mysteries of the Isles.
