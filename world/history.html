History

“A world still learning how to stand beneath a broken sky.”

The World Before

A History Written in Ruins

The modern Verdigris Isles are the remnants of a civilization transformed by magic, destroyed by its dependence upon it, and rebuilt by those who survived.

During the Age of Ascension, Aetherite powered both surface civilizations and great Ascended Cities that drifted across the skies. At the center of this age stood the Citadel, the first and only true flying nation in existence.

The Citadel was not merely a city. It was a sovereign nation suspended above the world, containing cities, towns, villages, farmland, forests, rivers, mines, and millions of inhabitants. For centuries, it followed vast aerial ley lines and harvested newly formed Aetherite from magical vents high above the earth.

The age eventually ended with the Sundering. As Aetherite reserves diminished and the systems built around them became unstable, the skyborne cities began disappearing one by one. Some vanished into storms. Others descended unexpectedly. Many were simply never seen again.

In the final collapse, the Citadel exhausted its remaining Aetherite reserves. Its Ascension Engine failed, and the entire nation fell from the sky. Its impact shattered an ancient continent into thousands of fragments, creating the scattered archipelago now known as the Verdigris Isles.

☁️ 02 · Age of Ascension

The Age That Reached the Sky

“Before kingdoms ruled the earth, one nation ruled the skies.”

The discovery of Aetherite, a crystal of pure magical essence found within a fallen star, changed civilization forever. Using this miraculous resource, the first Ascension Engine was created, allowing enormous landmasses to break free from the earth and rise into the heavens.

☄️

Discovery of Aetherite

Aetherite was discovered within the remains of a fallen star. The crystalline substance contained an extraordinary concentration of magical essence and reshaped the foundations of arcane study and technology across civilizations.

⚙️

Rise of Ascension Engines

Early experiments revealed that Aetherite could be harnessed as a sustained magical fuel source. The first Ascension Engine stabilized and channeled its power, eventually allowing entire structures and portions of terrain to rise from the earth.

☁️

The Sky Nations Era

Ascension technology advanced enough to raise entire landmasses into the sky. Ascended Cities became centers of government, scholarship, trade, and military power.

⚠️

Aetherite Decline

Natural Aetherite deposits began to diminish. Ascension Engines required increasing amounts of fuel to remain stable, causing several Ascended Cities to fail, vanish, or descend in uncontrolled collapses.

💥

The Sundering

As Aetherite became scarce and unstable, the global system of Ascension began to collapse. Atmospheric disturbances and ley line disruptions intensified, throwing the remaining Sky Nations into chaos.

☁️

The Fall of the Citadel

The Citadel became the final surviving Ascended Nation. When its final Aetherite reserves were exhausted, its Ascension Engine failed after centuries of continuous operation.

The Defining Catastrophe

The Fall of the Citadel

The Citadel was the first and only true flying nation. Unlike the Ascended Cities that remained politically tied to nations below, the Citadel was a sovereign nation suspended entirely in the sky.

Its fall marks Year 0 FC and remains the defining catastrophe of recorded history. The impact shattered an ancient continent into thousands of islands, permanently reshaping the world.

Year 0 FC The Citadel's final fall marks the beginning of the post-Fall calendar.
The Crownscar The impact left behind a vast ruin-field filled with unstable relics and remnants of Ascension technology.
The Isles The shattered continent became the fractured archipelago now known as the Verdigris Isles.

🌿 113 ER · Present Day

A World Still Rebuilding

“The world has not healed. It has adapted.”

A century has passed since the Fall of the Citadel. Civilization has rebuilt, but the consequences of the catastrophe remain visible throughout the Verdigris Isles.

🌊 The Verdigris Isles Today

  • Nations have formed from survivor settlements and reclaimed territories.
  • The Crownscar remains unstable and continues to produce relic energy and Aetherite anomalies.
  • Expeditions into Citadel ruins are dangerous, organized, and highly profitable.
  • Entire economies depend upon salvaging Ascension-era technology.
  • New generations have never seen a Sky City and know them primarily through stories and surviving records.

🧭 What 113 ER Means

  • The apocalypse is recent enough to still shape politics and culture.
  • Enough time has passed for myths and legends to develop.
  • Civilization has been rebuilt, but remains unstable in many regions.
  • Maps remain incomplete and contested.
  • The world is still being rediscovered.
