✦ The Modern Age

The World After Ascension

The modern nations of the Verdigris Isles exist within an era known as the Reclaimed Magitech Renaissance — a period of industrial growth, magical innovation, and rediscovery following the fall of the Citadel.

When the Ascension Era ended, civilization was forced to rebuild from scattered knowledge, salvaged relics, and practical ingenuity. Engineers, artificers, and scholars chose not to recreate the impossible wonders of the past. Instead, they forged a new path.

Steam power, precision engineering, metallurgy, railways, workshops, research guilds, and Drift-powered technology now define everyday life across much of the Isles.

It is an age of factories and foundries, locomotives and steamships, telegraph systems and mechanical workshops — a civilization steadily transforming itself without forgetting the ruins beneath its feet.

✦ Modern Power

Drift Changed Everything

The discovery of Aethersilt, commonly known as Drift, became the foundation of modern magitech.

🌫️ Refined Drift

A practical magical resource used to stabilize, regulate, and enhance modern machinery.

Magical Stabilization Industrial Power Infrastructure

Unlike the legendary Skyglass crystals of the Ascension Era, Drift cannot produce immense magical feats or sustain colossal machines.

Instead, Drift excels at regulating and stabilizing magical energy. This makes it ideal for practical, everyday applications.

Drift functions as the foundation of modern magitech, serving a role similar to electricity within an industrial society.

Public Lighting Communication Relays Medical Equipment Water Purification Heating Agriculture Industrial Equipment

Magic Does Not Replace Engineering

Every Drift-powered machine still requires skilled craftsmanship, mechanical components, maintenance, and trained operators. Magic enhances technology rather than replacing it.

✦ Icon of the Isles

Drift Airboats

Few inventions better represent the ingenuity of the modern age than the Drift airboat.

Designed specifically for the flooded landscapes of the Verdigris Isles, Drift airboats combine practical engineering with Drift stabilization technology.

Despite their name, they do not fly. A Drift Core reduces resistance against water, mud, reeds, flooded forests, and other difficult terrain while a powerful rear-mounted propeller provides forward thrust.

Their flat-bottomed construction allows them to navigate environments where conventional boats, wagons, and vehicles would quickly become stranded.

🌫️

Drift Core

A compact chamber filled with refined Drift creates a localized buoyancy field beneath the hull, reducing friction and making the vessel dramatically lighter without producing true flight.

🌀

Propulsion Fan

A large rear-mounted propeller pushes the vessel forward, allowing it to skim across wetlands, rivers, marshes, and other difficult terrain.

🛶

Flat Hull

The wide reinforced hull distributes weight evenly, allowing the vessel to travel across unstable terrain where conventional vehicles would sink.

Common Uses

Drift airboats are used throughout the Isles for crossing the Gatorback Marshes, navigating Mossfall Basin, reaching isolated villages, transporting supplies, conducting rescue operations, and supporting Crownscar expeditions.

⚠️ Cannot Fly Drift airboats remain surface vehicles and cannot achieve true aerial travel.
⚠️ Vulnerable Hull Rocky terrain and debris can damage their flat-bottomed construction.
⚠️ Exposed Propeller Dense forests and tight waterways require careful navigation.
⚠️ Drift Limitations Relic hazards may disrupt the Drift Core, which must periodically be recharged.
✦ Movement & Commerce

Transportation Across the Isles

Geography determines how people move. Water remains the foundation of travel, while rail and steam technology steadily reshape the land.

🌊

Waterways

Merchant vessels, sailing ships, steamships, riverboats, and Drift airboats connect distant communities through the extensive network of rivers, marshes, lakes, and coastal waters.

🚂

Railways

Rail transportation is becoming increasingly important near major cities and industrial centers, providing reliable connections for passengers, resources, and manufactured goods.

🐎

Rural Travel

Horse-drawn wagons remain common throughout rural settlements where railways have not yet reached.

☁️

The Lost Skies

True aerial travel remains beyond modern capabilities. Fragments of Ascension-era flight technology survive as dangerous relics, but no nation has successfully recreated the floating cities of the past.

✦ War in the Industrial Age

Military Technology

Modern warfare reflects industrial engineering as much as it reflects magic.

🔫

Firearms

Standing armies commonly field rifles, revolvers, breech-loading firearms, artillery, and specialized engineering equipment.

🛡️

Steel & Armor

Industrial metallurgy allows militaries to produce increasingly reliable steel armor, fortifications, weapons, and battlefield equipment.

✨

Magic on the Battlefield

Trained spellcasters and artificers serve alongside conventional soldiers, engineers, and support personnel, each contributing different capabilities.

🌫️

Drift Support

Drift is commonly used for logistics, communications, fortifications, and support equipment rather than functioning as a weapon itself.

⚙️

Engineering Corps

Engineers play a vital role in constructing defenses, maintaining machinery, managing transportation, and keeping military equipment operational.

🪄

Combined Doctrine

Modern armies increasingly combine firearms, magic, engineering, and industrial logistics into unified military strategies.

The greatest difference between the modern age and the Ascension Era is not intelligence or ambition. It is scale.

☁️

Ascension Era

Floating cities, Skyglass-powered engines, world-spanning magical networks, and machines whose principles remain largely impossible to understand.

⚙️

Modern Era

Railways, factories, steam engines, Drift-powered workshops, telegraph systems, industrial manufacturing, and practical magitech.
