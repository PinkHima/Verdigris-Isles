Magic & Magitech

The Aether Beneath Everything

Magic is part of the natural world, but using it requires knowledge, skill, and understanding of its limits.

✨

Aether

Aether exists throughout the world and forms the foundation of magical phenomena.

💎

Drift

A crystalline residue created by magical activity. Drift can collect, stabilize, and slowly release ambient Aether.

🔮

Aetherite

A rare and irreplaceable crystal once used to power the Ascension Engines of the old world.

The old technology

Magic became infrastructure.

The Ascension age transformed Aether from mystery into technology. Engines, cities, transport, and other systems were built around rare Aetherite.

When the crystal supply declined, the systems built around it became unstable. What remains is valuable, dangerous, and often poorly understood.

⚠️
Every relic is a shortcut to power—and a reminder that the old world paid a price for it.
